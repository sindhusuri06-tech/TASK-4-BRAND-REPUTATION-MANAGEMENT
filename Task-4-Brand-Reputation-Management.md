# TASK 4: Brand Reputation Management

## Brand Name
CodTech IT Solutions

## Objective
To monitor, manage, and improve the brand’s online reputation.

## Reputation Monitoring
- Monitor Google reviews
- Track social media comments
- Check mentions on forums and blogs

## Handling Negative Reviews
- Respond politely and professionally
- Acknowledge customer issues
- Provide solutions quickly
- Move conversation to private messages if needed

## Building Positive Reputation
- Encourage satisfied users to leave reviews
- Share testimonials on social media
- Post consistent and valuable content
- Engage actively with followers

## Tools Used
- Google Alerts
- Hootsuite
- Social media platforms

## Expected Outcome
- Improved brand trust
- Increased customer confidence
- Strong online presence
